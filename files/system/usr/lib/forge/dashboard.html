Release history

If an update breaks something

  1. Restart the PC and pick the previous Forge OS entry in the boot menu. Nothing is changed for good.
  2. To keep using that older version, run this and restart:
    rpm-ostree rollback
  3. To cancel an update that is ready but not started yet:
    rpm-ostree cleanup -p

Who can read which logs

Fedora keeps two kinds of journals. Your own user journal (your apps, and your Forge services such as backups and gaming mode) is always readable by you. The system journal (Forge Core, hardware checks, Ollama and the rest of the system) is readable by administrators in the wheel group, and by members of the adm or systemd-journal groups. To let another account read it: sudo usermod -aG systemd-journal NAME, then log out and in.
